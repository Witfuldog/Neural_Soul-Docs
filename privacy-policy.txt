NEURAL SOUL — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026/09/19
Applies to: Neural Soul (Steam, Windows)

Neural Soul is built to run on your own machine. This page explains
plainly what data exists, where it lives, and when anything ever leaves
your device.


1. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

any servers for it. The data it creates is stored locally on your own

While you are in a room, only these are shared with the other players in
that room: the name you use in the room, the room name, your character's
images and files, your voice level (quiet / talking / loud — never audio),
your expression and costume, which of the keys you bound to your character
are pressed, your character's placement, and the room's Pack. Nothing is
shared with anyone outside the room.

If you use a third-party virtual LAN tool for rooms (such as Radmin VPN or
Hamachi), its data handling is governed by that tool's own privacy policy.

Neural Soul contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no runtime AI
or machine-learning services, and it does not connect to any server
operated by us. The Unity engine it is built with sends diagnostic data to
Unity, as described in Section 4.


3. DISCORD GUEST MODE

This optional feature lets a room host show friends from their Discord
voice call as characters. Nothing happens until the host sets it up and
starts it.

Neural Soul learns only who is in that voice channel, their display
names, whether they are muted, and when each of them starts and stops
transmitting voice. It never listens to, decodes, records or transcribes
the call, and never reads your messages.

Member names and speaking states are used only to show characters on the
host's screen and in the host's OBS output. They are not saved, and they
are not sent to us or to other players in the room. The only things saved
are described in Section 1. Discord itself is governed by Discord's
privacy policy.

How it connects: the host creates their own Discord Bot, invites it to
their server and pastes its token into Neural Soul. A helper program on
your computer then signs in as your Bot and joins the voice channel you
pick, so everyone in the call can see that it is there. It joins muted
and never transmits. Voice packets from the call are end-to-end encrypted
and Neural Soul does not decrypt them; it only notices that packets are
arriving, which is how it knows who is currently transmitting. Your Bot
token is used only to sign that Bot in; it stays on your computer, is
never sent to us, and is only stored if you choose to remember it, as
described in Section 1.


4. ENGINE DIAGNOSTICS (UNITY)

Neural Soul is built with the Unity engine, which automatically sends
diagnostic data to Unity Technologies. This is information about the
stability and performance of the app on your hardware and software, such
as crash logs, engine telemetry and configuration details. We use the
resulting reports only to find and fix crashes and performance problems.
Unity processes this data under its own privacy policy:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy


5. MICROPHONE, KEYBOARD AND MUSIC

Microphone   Analyzed on your computer to decide whether your character
             is quiet, talking or loud. Audio is never recorded or sent
             anywhere; in rooms, only the level is shared.

Keyboard     Neural Soul only checks the keys you have bound to actions
             and its own shortcuts. It does not record anything you type
             in other programs.

Music        To react to music, Neural Soul reads the currently playing
             track from Windows media controls and analyzes the music
             app's audio on your computer. None of this is sent anywhere.


6. YOUR CONTROL OVER YOUR DATA

Deleting the folder listed in Section 1 permanently removes your settings,
your custom characters and the Discord guest layout. Uninstalling through
Steam does not remove this folder, so delete it yourself if you want
everything gone. You can end Discord guest mode at any time with
"Disconnect Discord". You can also clear the remembered token in Neural
Soul, remove the Bot from your server, or reset its token in the Discord
Developer Portal.


7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Neural Soul is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal data from children.


8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes — for example, if a future feature introduces a new
type of network connection — this page will be updated and the "Last
updated" date at the top will change accordingly.
