NEURAL SOUL — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: 2026/07/25
Applies to: Neural Soul (Steam, Windows)

Neural Soul is built to run entirely on your own machine. This page explains
plainly what data exists, where it lives, and when anything ever leaves
your device.


1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Neural Soul does not collect, upload, or transmit any personally
identifiable information. The only data that exists is stored locally on
your own device and never leaves it, unless you use an online feature
described in Section 3.

  Data type                          Purpose                          Stored where
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AI chat history                    Maintains AI memory/context      Local JSON file
  User settings (volume, language)   Restores your preferences        Local PlayerPrefs
                                                                       (Windows Registry)
  Stickers / Avatar images           Displays your custom character   Local folder


2. WHERE YOUR CONVERSATION DATA LIVES

Chat data is stored as plain JSON files on your own machine. You can open
these folders and delete them at any time — nothing is mirrored anywhere
else.

  AI conversation context:
  C:\Users\{Username}\AppData\LocalLow\sili guy studio\Neural Soul\UserData\neural_soul_avatars\{AvatarName}\chat_history.json

  Chat UI display log:
  C:\Users\{Username}\AppData\LocalLow\sili guy studio\Neural Soul\UserData\neural_soul_avatars\{AvatarName}\chat_ui_history.json

  AI long-term memory summary:
  C:\Users\{Username}\AppData\LocalLow\sili guy studio\Neural Soul\UserData\neural_soul_avatars\{AvatarName}\chat_memory.json


3. NETWORK CONNECTIONS

Most of Neural Soul runs fully offline. Below is every situation where any
network connection is made, and exactly what is sent.

  Feature           Connects to              What happens
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  AI chat           http://127.0.0.1         Talks to the llama-server process
                     (local)                 running on your own machine.
                                              Nothing leaves your device.

  Steam Workshop    Steam's servers          Used only to subscribe to and
                                              download stickers/avatars made by
                                              other creators, handled entirely
                                              by the Steamworks SDK under
                                              Steam's own privacy policy.

  LAN Party         Other players' devices   You connect using a third-party
  connection        (peer-to-peer)           virtual LAN tool of your choice
                                              (e.g. Radmin VPN, Hamachi). While
                                              connected, only your chosen
                                              sticker image and a true/false
                                              "microphone active" flag are
                                              shared between players — no
                                              audio stream and no personally
                                              identifying data is transmitted.

  Note: any data handling performed by third-party tools you choose to use
  for LAN Party connections (such as Radmin VPN or Hamachi) is governed by
  that tool's own privacy policy, not by Neural Soul.


4. YOUR CONTROL OVER YOUR DATA

Because everything lives in local files, you are always in control.
Deleting the folders listed in Section 2 permanently removes your chat
history and memory. Uninstalling the application removes settings and
local avatar/sticker assets.


5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Neural Soul is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect
any data from anyone, regardless of age, since no data is collected or
transmitted in the first place.


6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes — for example, if a future feature introduces a new
type of network connection — this page will be updated and the "Last
updated" date at the top will change accordingly.
